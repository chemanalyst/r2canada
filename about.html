About

Why this site exists

Rivian's Canadian rollout is scattered across press releases, forum threads and quietly-edited web pages. R2 Canada Tracker pulls it into one place.

R2 Canada Tracker is an independent resource for Rivian owners and reservation holders in Canada. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Rivian Automotive, Inc.

The goal is simple: track what's actually confirmed about Rivian's presence in Canada — pricing, delivery timelines, service coverage, and charging infrastructure — separate from speculation, and give owners a place to compare real-world experience with their vehicles.

How information is sourced

News summaries link back to original reporting and Rivian's own public statements where possible. Dates and figures are marked as US-market reference points when Canadian-specific data isn't yet available — treat anything without a Canadian source as unconfirmed for this market.

Who's behind this

I'm Eli — an EV skeptic turned EV enthusiast, pretty recently. I got hooked the moment I sat in an R1S, but it was way too expensive for me to actually buy one. So now I'm doing the next best thing: waiting for the R2 to land in Canada, and building the resource I wish existed while I wait. If you're in the same boat, this site's for you.

Get in touch

Spotted something out of date? Let me know on Facebook. Have your own reservation and delivery dates to share? Add them on the reservation tracker.
