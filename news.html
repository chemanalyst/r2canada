R2Feb 2026

Canadian R2 launch pushed from 2026 to 2027, price pulled from site

Rivian quietly updated its Canadian R2 page: the delivery window slipped a full year and the previously listed C$66,500 starting price disappeared entirely.

Why Canada is behind the US on R2. The R2 is assembled in Illinois and, at launch, ships to Canada subject to Canadian counter-tariffs on US-built autos — a structural cost disadvantage Rivian has flagged with no near-term fix.
