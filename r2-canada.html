Reference

The R2, mapped for the Canadian market

Everything currently known: the delivery timeline, US specs and pricing as a reference point, and what's still unconfirmed for Canada.

Status as of Jul 2026: Canadian deliveries are targeted for 2027. No Canadian pricing has been published since the original C$66,500 figure was removed in February 2026. Treat every date on this page as subject to change — this is a tracking page, not an official source.
The route so far

Full timeline: promise to revised course

01
2024

R2 unveiled — Canadian price listed at C$66,500

Rivian opens Canadian reservations ($150 CAD deposit) with a first-half-2026 delivery target and a published starting price.

02
2025

Production ramps toward saleable units

Rivian enters saleable production on R2 Performance, the trim that will lead the US launch. Canadian timeline still shows 2026 through this period.

03
Feb 2026

Canadian timeline slips a full year, price pulled

Rivian quietly updates the Canadian R2 page: delivery window moves from 2026 to 2027, and the C$66,500 figure is removed with no replacement. The company cites focus on the US launch; independent reporting also points to new Canadian counter-tariffs on US-built vehicles as a factor, since the R2 is assembled in Illinois.

04
Mar 12, 2026

US configurator, full specs and pricing go live

Performance ($57,990 USD, spring 2026), Premium ($53,990 USD, late 2026) and Standard ($48,490–45,000 USD, 2027) trims are priced for US buyers. Canada is not included in this reveal.

05
Apr 2026

Saleable Performance production confirmed

Rivian confirms R2 Performance has entered saleable production, matching the Tesla Model Y Performance on energy consumption while offering more range.

06
Jul 2026

First-half 2027 order invites confirmed for Canada

Canadian reservation holders are told to expect order invites in the first half of 2027, ahead of deliveries. Canadian pricing remains unannounced.

Confirmed for Canada — Aug 2026

Trim lineup

Rivian's Canadian site (rivian.com/en-CA/r2) now lists all three trims with Canadian range and performance figures — but still no price. US pricing is shown alongside for reference only; it hasn't been confirmed for the Canadian market.

Note: the Canadian site lists Standard as a single trim with RWD and Long Range as configuration options, rather than as two separately priced tiers like the US site.
